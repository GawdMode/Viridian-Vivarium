# Viridian Vivarium

**Viridian Vivarium** is a Pokémon Crystal Gen1Recomp gameplay mod that turns Route 2 into a small conservation and restoration project centered around Bug- and plant-associated Pokémon.

Meet **Jaxen** and **Fern**, hunt for Pokémon with the **Field Net**, donate specimens toward conservation goals, grow berries and fungi, care for residents in the Vivarium, unlock new shop stock, and eventually raise special **Prime Pokémon**.

## Features

### Field Net hunting
Use the **Field Net** near tall grass to scan the surrounding area. Grass may begin to rustle; walk directly into a rustling patch to begin the catching minigame.

Field Net encounters use habitat-specific tables and time-of-day preferences, so where and when you hunt matters without hard-locking species to a single period.

The mod includes **29 eligible conservation species**, and every one of them can also live in the Vivarium.

### Conservation donations
Use the Vivarium **Logbook** to donate eligible Pokémon toward species quotas.

- **Common species:** 30 donations
- **Uncommon species:** 15 donations
- **Rare species:** 10 donations

Completing Common quotas alternates new shop unlocks between Jaxen and Fern. Uncommon and Rare quotas later award species-themed vanilla item rewards, with an additional reward for completing the full Rare tier.

### Jaxen's shop
Jaxen focuses on hunting and Pokémon development.

- **Scent Spray** — increases rustling-grass frequency for 100 overworld steps without changing species rarity.
- **Sparkle Bits** — scatter them across the Vivarium for a chance to transform one resident. A normal Pokémon may become shiny; a shiny Pokémon may become **Prime**.
- **Shiny Net** — a one-time upgraded net purchase with improved shiny odds. It is used just like the Field Net and is marked **SOLD** after purchase.

### Fern's shop
Fern focuses on cultivation.

- **Fungi Spores** — plant them to grow mushrooms that can yield TinyMushroom or Big Mushroom.
- **Berry Seed** — grows into a random vanilla Berry.
- **Fertilizer** — increases a fertilized crop's harvest from the normal 1 item to 1–3 items.

### Vivarium habitat
The Vivarium can house eligible conservation Pokémon and acts as the center of the mod's care loop.

- Bug and plant residents react differently to the habitat lighting.
- **Lights ON** favor plant growth.
- **Lights OFF** favor fungi.
- The vanilla **Squirt Bottle** can be used to water planted plots and speed their growth.
- Plants, mushrooms, resident movement, and ambient effects are rendered inside the habitat view.

### Shiny and Prime Pokémon
**Sparkle Bits** can advance Vivarium residents through a special progression:

**Normal → Shiny → Prime**

Prime Pokémon:
- use a custom **gold palette**
- keep shiny battle sparkle behavior
- display a custom **Prime symbol** instead of the normal shiny symbol
- receive a **+40 total base-stat boost**
- retain Prime status through evolution
- keep their Prime presentation outside the Vivarium, including battle and summary displays

### Advanced conservation rewards
After the main shop progression is open, completing Uncommon and Rare quotas gives one-time species-themed vanilla rewards from Jaxen.

Finishing the entire Rare tier awards an additional conservation completion bundle.

## Installation

1. Install a compatible version of **gen1recomp** for Pokémon Crystal.
2. Place `Viridian-Vivarium-v1.0.0.zip` with your other Gen1Recomp mods.
3. Enable **Viridian Vivarium** in the mod menu.
4. Load Pokémon Crystal and visit **Route 2**.

The mod is designed for the Pokémon Crystal (USA/Europe Rev 1) Gen1Recomp environment used during development.

## Compatibility

Viridian Vivarium includes compatibility handling for shared Kanto map/tileset changes used by other mods in this project and optional support for **Unique Menu Icons**.

Because large gameplay mods can touch the same engine or map systems, keep backups of important saves when combining major mods.

## Save compatibility

The 1.0 package intentionally preserves the development build's internal mod/save identifiers so saves created with the late DEV builds can continue forward without losing Vivarium progression.
